Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

ProjectFriendly does not directly collect, store, sell, or share personal information from its users. The only external account-related functionality used by the game is Google Play Games sign-in, which is used to associate and save the player's game progress.

1. Information We Collect

ProjectFriendly does not directly collect any personal information from users.

The Game does not request or collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Precise location
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Payment information
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Advertising identifiers for personalized advertising
  • Other personally identifiable information

The Game does not contain forms or other mechanisms designed to directly collect personal information from users.

2. Google Play Games

ProjectFriendly may use Google Play Games services to allow players to sign in and associate their game progress with their Google Play Games account.

Google Play Games sign-in is used only for game-related functionality, primarily to identify the player's game profile and allow their game progress to be saved and restored.

ProjectFriendly does not intentionally access or collect additional personal information from the player's Google account beyond what is necessary for this functionality.

The Game does not use Google Play Games sign-in to create advertising profiles, provide personalized advertising, or track users for marketing purposes.

3. Game Progress

When Google Play Games sign-in is used, information associated with the player's game progress may be stored through the services used by the Game.

This information is used solely to provide gameplay functionality, including:

  • Saving game progress
  • Restoring game progress
  • Associating progress with the player's Google Play Games profile
  • Allowing players to continue their progress across supported sessions or devices

ProjectFriendly does not use game progress information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or selling information to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

ProjectFriendly does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

The Game only relies on third-party services when necessary to provide its intended functionality, such as Google Play Games for authentication and game progress functionality.

ProjectFriendly does not use personal information for targeted advertising or user profiling.

5. Analytics and Advertising

ProjectFriendly does not intentionally collect personal information for analytics, behavioral profiling, or advertising purposes.

The Game does not use personal information to provide personalized advertisements.

6. Children's Privacy

ProjectFriendly does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the Game does not directly collect personal information, users can play the Game without providing personal information directly to ProjectFriendly.

Parents and guardians should be aware that Google Play Games is a separate service operated by Google and may have its own policies and requirements.

7. Data Security

Since ProjectFriendly does not directly collect or maintain users' personal information, it does not maintain a database of users' personal information.

Any information handled by third-party services, such as Google Play Games, is subject to the security practices and privacy policies of those services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to ProjectFriendly or its services.

If this Privacy Policy is changed, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of ProjectFriendly, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
